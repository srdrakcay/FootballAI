Privacy Policy

1. Scope and controller

This policy applies to the NinetyLens iOS app with bundle identifier com.footballai.predictions.com. Privacy requests can be submitted through the Support page linked below.

2. Data we process

The app stores a Firebase-generated anonymous user identifier, system locale, account creation and last-seen times, credit balance and usage, favorite matches, purchase records, and analysis history. Firebase and infrastructure providers may process IP addresses, device-integrity signals, and technical logs for security. NinetyLens does not access precise location or collect advertising identifiers.

3. Purposes

Data is used to maintain the anonymous session, restore account-linked content, enforce credit usage, display fixtures, validate purchases, prevent abuse, and secure the service.

4. Providers

Firebase/Google Cloud provides authentication, database, server functions, remote configuration, and app-integrity services. football-data.org supplies match data through server-side requests and does not receive the anonymous user identifier. RevenueCat processes anonymous account, product, and transaction information to validate one-time credit purchases. NinetyLens does not sell personal data or use it for behavioral advertising or cross-service tracking.

5. Retention and deletion

Account-linked data is retained until account deletion. Delete Account removes the anonymous Firebase account and linked profile data, including credits, favorites, history, and user-linked request records. Shared match analyses containing no user identifier may be retained for service integrity. Signing out removes only the session from the device.

6. Security and transfers

Data is encrypted in transit and protected using authentication, Firebase Security Rules, and app-integrity controls. Cloud providers may process data outside your country. No technical measure can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices

You can remove favorites, sign out, or permanently delete your account and associated data from the app. Applicable access, correction, deletion, or objection requests can be submitted through Support.

8. Children and changes

NinetyLens is not specifically directed to children and does not knowingly collect names, email addresses, or birth dates. This policy may be updated when data practices change.
