# NinetyLens App Store Strategy

## Recommended name

### 1. NinetyLens

Recommended store titles:

- English (US): `NinetyLens: Football AI`
- Turkish: `NinetyLens: Futbol Analizi`

Why it leads:

- Distinctive enough to build a recognizable brand instead of competing only on the generic “Football AI” phrase.
- “Ninety” immediately connects to a football match without using a club, league, or federation trademark.
- “Lens” supports the product promise: helping supporters inspect a match through data, probabilities, context, favorites, and history.
- The localized title and subtitle can still carry the strongest functional search terms.

Before final submission, perform a trademark check in the intended launch territories. Search-result availability is not legal clearance.

## Name alternatives

| Rank | Name | Strength | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | NinetyLens | Memorable, football-specific, supports a premium visual identity | Requires a small initial brand-building effort |
| 2 | TactiScore AI | Immediately sounds football/data oriented | “Tactical” can overstate the current depth of the product |
| 3 | FootballAI | Perfect continuity with the codebase and existing icon | Generic, crowded, difficult to own in search |
| 4 | MatchMetric AI | Strong analytics meaning | Less emotional and has unrelated web/project usage |

Names intentionally excluded after research:

- MatchLens AI: already used by football-analysis products.
- PitchMind: already used by football scouting and unrelated AI products.
- PitchSignal: already used by a football prediction service.
- FormaIQ / FormPulse / Matchory: active brands in other product categories.

## Positioning

Primary promise:

> A calm, data-informed match companion that turns fixtures and team context into transparent AI-assisted probabilities.

Product principles for store messaging:

- Say “analysis,” “insights,” and “probabilities.”
- Avoid “guaranteed,” “accurate winner,” “winning tips,” “best prediction,” and similar unverifiable claims.
- Avoid betting, odds, coupons, or bookmaker language.
- State that analyses are informational, probabilistic, and not betting advice.
- Emphasize the no-subscription, one-time credit model without putting regional prices in metadata.
